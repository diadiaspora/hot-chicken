Wing FAQ

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver locally. Message us on Facebook Messenger with your address to confirm availability and delivery time.

Can I mix flavors?

Absolutely. Order one flavor at a time through the cards, or message us to create a mixed-wing order.

What sizes can I order?

We offer 6, 12, and 24-wing portions. Larger catering orders can be arranged through Messenger.

How spicy are the wings?

Flavors range from sweet and mild to extremely hot. Each card includes a heat level so you can choose your comfort zone.
